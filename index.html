Dialogs on the browser's own dialog

Vitrine modal adds what <dialog> lacks: entry and exit transitions, a backdrop click and Escape that close (each optional), the focus back where it was, a scroll lock that doesn't move the page, modals over modals, a sheet on phones, and the Back button closing the top one. No dependencies, no build step.

<button data-modal-open="about">About</button>
<dialog id="about" class="vitrine-modal" data-size="sm" aria-labelledby="about-title">
  <h2 id="about-title">About</h2> …
  <button data-modal-close>Close</button>
</dialog>

Options, events and theming with --vitrine-modal-* custom properties: the README. Pictures too? Vitrine, the lightbox, shares the same core.

Vitrine modal is free software under the GPL-3.0-or-later.

Small

Escape, a click beside it, Back or the button close it, and the focus goes back to what opened it.

Large

Modals stack on the browser's top layer. Open another: Escape closes only the top one.

Sheet

On a phone this rises from the bottom edge; on a wider screen it is a centred 560 px dialog.
