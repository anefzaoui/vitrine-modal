# Changelog

The format follows [Keep a Changelog](https://keepachangelog.com/en/1.1.0/); versions follow
[Semantic Versioning](https://semver.org/).

## [1.0.0] - unreleased

The first version, modals on the native dialog element: transitions, stacking, a phone sheet, the Back button, a scroll lock that doesn't move the page. It began as the lightbox and dialogs of
misterdownloadmanager.com, then of minecraft-server-manager.com and Jessbot's site, and was
made a project of its own. `vitrine-core.js` 1.0.0 is shared with
[anefzaoui/vitrine](https://github.com/anefzaoui/vitrine).
